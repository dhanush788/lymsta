Privacy

What this site collects.

Last updated 14 October 2026

lymsta.com is a static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and has no accounts, no forms and no tracking of any kind. We do not build a profile of you, and we have nothing to sell to anyone.

What we collect directly

Nothing. There is no JavaScript on this site, no analytics script, no advertising pixel, no cookie banner because there are no cookies, and no form to fill in. We do not know who you are or that you visited.

What our hosting provider sees

This site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the requests your browser makes, which includes your IP address, and it may log them for security and operational reasons. That happens at the hosting layer, before anything reaches us, and we have no access to those logs.

GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement and, specifically for this case, their note on data collection on GitHub Pages.

Fonts

The typeface on this site is loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes a request to Google's servers, and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. We do not receive anything from this and we pass nothing to Google ourselves. Their terms are described in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

We mention this because it is the only third party your browser contacts when loading this page, and we would rather name it than let it go unsaid.

If you email us

Writing to hello@lymsta.com means we hold your message and your address, because that is what email is. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not pass it on.

If you want your message and address deleted, ask and we will delete them.

Our apps

This policy covers this website only. Lymsta has not released any apps yet. When one ships, it will carry its own privacy policy describing what that specific app collects, and that policy will be linked from its store listing and from this site.

Do not read this page as a statement about a future app. An app that handles accounts or user data will have obligations this static site simply does not.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. There is no version history beyond that, though the site's full edit history is public on GitHub if you ever want to check what changed and when.

Contact

Questions about any of the above go to hello@lymsta.com.
