Approach

How the studio works

  • 01

    We start from our own problems

    Every product begins with something we wanted to exist and could not find. If we would not open it daily ourselves, we do not build it.

  • 02

    One team, end to end

    Design, code and support are not separate stages passed between hands. A product is carried from first sketch to shipped build by the same people.

  • 03

    Every platform from the start

    Web, Android and iOS are planned together rather than ported one after another once the first one works. A product ships when all of its platforms are ready.

  • 04

    Independent, and staying that way

    No clients. What ships is what we think should ship, and we live with the consequences of getting it wrong.
